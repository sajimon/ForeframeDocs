# Bubble Counter CO₂ Support

Welcome to the support page for **Bubble Counter CO₂**.

Bubble Counter CO₂ is an iPhone app that uses the camera to count CO₂ bubbles and measure their flow rate.

## Contact

For support, bug reports, or feedback, please contact:

**neural_dot51@icloud.com**

## What to include in your message

To help us understand and resolve your issue faster, please include:

- device model,

- iOS version,

- app version,

- short description of the problem,

- steps to reproduce the issue,

- screenshots, if relevant.

## Feedback
