# Foreframe Support

Welcome to the support page for **Foreframe**.

Foreframe is a field scouting and framing tool for photographers who want to evaluate composition, focal length, sensor format, and aspect ratio before taking the final shot with their main camera.

## Contact

For support, bug reports, or feedback, please contact:

**neural_dot51@icloud.com**

## What to include in your message

To help us understand and resolve your issue faster, please include:

- device model,

- iOS version,

- app version,

- short description of the problem,

- steps to reproduce the issue,

- screenshots, if relevant.

## Feedback

We also welcome feature requests and general feedback.  

If you have ideas that could improve Foreframe, feel free to send them by email.
